<h2>What I work on</h2>

<p align="center">
Microelectronics undergraduate at the <b>Karlsruhe Institute of Technology</b>, working as a research
assistant at the <b>Institute of Microstructure Technology</b> (IMT, Korvink group) on superconducting
Lenz-lens resonators for NMR signal enhancement.
<br><br>
Alongside that I build open-source tooling that brings physics-based process models, machine learning
and conversational AI together for lithography, oxidation, implantation, defect yield and GDSII layout analysis.
</p>
